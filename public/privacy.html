Privacy

The short version: your camera never reaches our servers, you have no account, and the things we do keep exist only to run and moderate the service.

What never touches our servers

Video and audio. They travel peer-to-peer, directly between the two browsers in a session, encrypted with DTLS-SRTP. There is no server-side recording of sessions, live or after the fact.

Recordings you make. If you use the record control, capture happens locally in your browser. You choose to save or delete it; we never receive it.

What is processed to make the service work

  • Connection data. Your IP address is handled transiently to deliver connections, count who is online, and rate-limit abuse. It is not turned into profiles.
  • Text messages. They are relayed through the server so they arrive in real time, in the same session as the video.
  • Reports. When you report someone, the report and its reason are stored so a moderator can review and act on it. This is the one case where content is kept, because moderation without it is theatre.
  • Ad delivery. We serve ads ourselves and record that an ad was shown or clicked, with a coarse timestamp, so advertisers can be billed accurately.
  • Contact messages. Anything you send through the contact page reaches the team and is kept until it has been dealt with.

Local storage

Small preferences may be kept in your own browser, on your own device. Clearing your browser data removes them. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not run third-party trackers on this site.

Your choices

There is no account to delete, because there are no accounts. To ask about data connected to an IP address or to send a privacy question, use the contact page. The service is for ages 18 and over.

Changes

If what we collect changes materially, this page changes with it. The date below is when it was last reviewed.
