ABOUT US

About LELA

A random video chat built the boring way: your camera goes to one person, not to us, and someone reads every report.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Small on purpose, serious where it counts.

Why it exists

Talking to a stranger used to be a normal thing on the internet. Most services that offered it either shut down, added walls of sign-up forms, or quietly started recording. LELA keeps the original promise — open the page, meet one person — and fixes the parts that made those services unpleasant: the accounts, the queues of profiles, and the absence of anyone in charge when something goes wrong.

OUR MISSION

Keep the original promise.

It is deliberately small in scope. One stranger at a time, camera and text, a Next button and a Report button. Nothing to configure and nothing to swipe.

IN PRACTICE

How moderation works

Every session carries a report control with reasons attached. A report does not vanish into a filter — it lands in an admin queue where a person opens it, checks the account history, and acts: a warning, a removal from the session, or a ban. Repeat offenders are banned. Blocks take effect immediately, without review, because in the moment you should not have to argue for them.

What we never do

  • No server-side recording. Video and audio travel directly between the two browsers. The server introduces you and then steps out of the room.
  • No accounts. No email, no password, no profile, nothing to delete later because nothing was kept.
  • No paywall. There is no premium tier and no paid feature held back.

The full rules live in the community guidelines. They are short on purpose so they can actually be read.

If you record an encounter yourself, that recording stays on your own device and is yours to keep or remove.

Say hello

Questions, bug reports, or press — the contact page reaches the team. For anything that happened inside a session, use the report button instead; those are read first.
